Who I Am

I'm Austin Cheng, a Grade 10 student at KCIS taking Robotics Foundations I: Robot Modeling this year. I'm curious about how machines are designed before they're ever built — the math, the motion, and the modeling that turn an idea into something that can move on its own.

This site is where I'm tracking that process: what I'm learning each session, the models I build, and the evidence that shows how far I've come by June.

What Robot Modeling covers

A working map of the topics I'll build my log and journal entries around this year.

CAD & Robot Modeling

Building digital models of robot parts and mechanisms before anything gets built.

Kinematics & Motion

How joints, links, and gears translate into predictable, controllable movement.

Sensors & Perception

Giving a robot a way to read its environment — distance, light, orientation.

Actuators & Control

Motors, servos, and the logic that turns a decision into a physical action.

Programming Logic

Writing the code that gives a modeled robot its behavior.

Testing & Iteration

Checking models against reality, then rebuilding what doesn't hold up.
